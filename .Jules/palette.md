## 2026-09-27 - Accessibility and semantics enhancements
**Learning:** Pagination controls often lack semantic context, requiring <nav aria-label="Pagination"> wraps and descriptive aria-labels for buttons. Icon-only elements like close buttons (&times;) must include `aria-label` and `title` to ensure they're understandable and accessible.
**Action:** Enhance UI accessibility and semantics by consistently wrapping paginations in semantically meaningful tags with aria labels and ensure all icon-only buttons clearly explain their function to all users.
