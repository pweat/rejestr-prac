## 2026-10-28 - Accessible Pagination Pattern
**Learning:** Reusable pagination components must use semantic `<nav>` tags with appropriate `aria-label` (e.g., "Nawigacja po stronach"), provide Polish `aria-label`s for icon-only previous/next buttons, and use `aria-current="page"` to indicate the active page for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always ensure pagination controls are wrapped in `<nav>` and use ARIA attributes correctly for localized accessibility.
